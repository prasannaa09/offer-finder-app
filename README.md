# Offer Finder

Compare today's live **Zomato** and **Swiggy** delivery offers near you, side by side, and see which app is cheaper for each restaurant. Filter by the exact offers you want (50% off, 70% off, flat ₹ off…), rating, distance and pure veg, with coupon codes shown. Android app.

## Download

**➡️ [Download the latest APK](https://github.com/prasannaa09/offer-finder-app/releases/latest)**  (Android 8.0+)

## Install
1. Download `offer-finder-<version>.apk` from the link above.
2. Tap the file. If Android asks, allow installing from this source.
3. If Google Play Protect warns, tap **More details -> Install anyway**. On stricter phones (MIUI/Xiaomi, some Samsung) you may need to briefly turn off Play Protect scanning (Play Store -> profile -> Play Protect -> Settings), install, then turn it back on.
4. Open the app, connect **Zomato** (and **Swiggy**, to compare) with your phone number + OTP, and pick a saved delivery address.

Updating from an older version: install over it. You may need to log in again.

## How it works
- Offers start loading as soon as you pick an address, and nearby restaurants show up first.
- The list keeps itself fresh while the app is open. **Check for new offers** shows when the next check is possible.
- Each restaurant card shows its Zomato offer next to its Swiggy offers (including Swiggy coupon codes and bank offers), with the better deal marked **BEST**.
- Tap **Open in Zomato** / **Open in Swiggy** to order in that app.

## Privacy
There is no server. The app talks to Zomato and Swiggy directly from your own phone, and your logins are stored only on your device.
